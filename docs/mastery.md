## Reading the Rungs column

Four characters, one per rung, always in this order. A completed rung shows
its letter; an outstanding one shows `·`.

| Position | Letter | Rung | Cleared by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `D` | Derive | a lesson — reproduce the formula, name each term |
| 2 | `A` | Apply | a scenario or notebook — use it on real numbers |
| 3 | `B` | Break | a failure lab — find the flaw in a plausible wrong use |
| 4 | `E` | Explain | a teach-back — defend it to a sceptical PM, no notes |

So `····` is untouched, `DA··` has been derived and applied, and `DABE` is
complete. `D·B·` is legal and means exactly what it says: derived and broken,
never applied, never explained.

Status follows from the rungs rather than being set independently: any rung
cleared makes a concept `weak`, all four make it `confirmed`. A concept with
`····` is `untested`.
